# Story Dungeon: Beginner Playtest Worksheet

**Blank worksheet: no human participants, observations, scores, or learning results are recorded here.** Automated checks are technical evidence, not human playtesting.

Use one copy per participant. ให้ผู้ทดสอบลองเล่นจริง บันทึกสิ่งที่เห็น ไม่กรอกผลแทนผู้เล่น และเว้นช่องไว้หากยังไม่ได้ทดสอบ

## Purpose

Find out whether a beginner can understand the short situations, build useful messages from provided phrases, explain what those messages mean, and finish without frustration. Separately record interface or technical problems. The activities assess supported comprehension and phrase selection, not independent writing or a certified language level.

## Opening explanation

> We are testing the game, not giving you a grade. Try each task and tell us if something is confusing. You can use the Thai meanings and the game's help. If you need to stop, that is fine. We want to make the game easier and clearer.

Observe first. If facilitator help is needed, give it and record exactly what was said. Distinguish **completed using the game's built-in support** from **completed after facilitator help**. Even without facilitator help, the supplied phrases are still language support.

## Meaning evidence: more than clicking

Ask for an explanation after the player has sent a message. They may explain the meaning in Thai or simple English; record which language and support they used. Do not silently improve their wording.

|---|---|

| Which words or parts remained confusing? | __________ |
| Thai translations, listening, retry hints, or facilitator help used | __________ |
| Difference between game completion and observed understanding | __________ |

Useful questions:

- What are you asking Tali to do?
- Why is this reply helpful here?
- Can you point to the phrase that tells your location or describes the problem?
- In the directions message, what happens first and what should your partner avoid?
- Why should you wait for RECEIVED before turning off the radio?

Choosing between two useful phrases is supported language practice. It does not prove that the player could invent the message independently. Record partial understanding as partial understanding.

## Partner directions activity

Draw a simple map matching the mission: a left raindrop path, a blue pillar straight ahead, a right turn, and eye tiles to avoid. One learner reads or shows their English message; a partner follows it with a finger or counter. Keep it seated and simple.

| Field | Actual observation |
|---|---|

| Partner's first movement, next movement, and turn | __________ |
| Did the partner avoid the eye tiles? | __________ |
| Clarification requested or Thai support used | __________ |
| Could the learner explain a misunderstanding? | __________ |

This activity tests whether the message can guide an action and whether its sender understands it. Do not attribute success to the game alone when the partner or facilitator supplied extra help.

## Optional new-situation check

Try a short, age-appropriate situation after the game, such as asking a teammate for help finding a pencil or describing a door that will not open. Invite a spoken phrase, gesture plus phrase, or supported phrase choice. Do not require an essay.

Check these where relevant:

- Short text, large enough buttons, and no horizontal scrolling on the tested screen.
- English phrases and Thai meanings remain visible in either interface language.
- An unhelpful dialogue reply allows a friendly retry; the gold-star story recommendation is enabled.
- Missing message parts cannot be sent as a completed task, and sending all parts gives Tali's response.
- Health stays full even after a risky story choice.
- Reload restores beginner mission choices when local browser storage is available.
- An older learning-version-one save keeps its story route but starts the new beginner missions.
- Journal download contains the selected phrase message, without claiming original writing.
- Listening works only if an installed local English voice is available; reading still works otherwise.
- The final choice starts the 3D celebration automatically unless the skip-ending preference is on; record whether 3D, lighter effects, or the 2D fallback was used.
